Good to know

Can I buy more than one artwork?

Absolutely. Add as many pieces as you like to your inquiry list and they'll all be included in your text message.

Are these prints?

No — every listing is the one-of-a-kind original on paper. Most are signed and dated by the artist.

Do the artworks come framed?

Works are sold unframed, ready for a mat and frame of your choice. Each listing shows the exact sheet size in centimeters and inches.

How are payment and delivery handled?

Everything is arranged personally by text after you reach out. Shipped pieces are packed flat between rigid boards to keep the paper safe.
