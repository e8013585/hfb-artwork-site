About the work

Bold color, fast brushwork, big feeling.

HFB paints and draws on paper with whatever the moment calls for — thick acrylic and gouache, glowing watercolor washes, oil pastel, charcoal, ink and marker. The results range from roaring characters and wildlife to moody cityscapes, still lifes and pure abstraction.

Most pieces in this collection were created in 2015 and are signed and dated by hand. Each is a single original — once it's gone, it's gone.
